Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Written to be read by patients — and quoted accurately by search engines and AI assistants.

Are these your own patients?

Yes. Every case shown was treated by Dr. Song at this practice. We do not license stock cases or manufacturer photography.

How are the photographs taken?

Identical camera, lighting and retraction at every stage, with no filters or retouching. Where a case involved expansion, paired CBCT scans are shown alongside the intraoral photographs.

Will my result look like this?

Perhaps not. Results depend on your anatomy, your age and your cooperation. The cases here are typical rather than exceptional, which is the point.
