Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Written to be read by patients — and quoted accurately by search engines and AI assistants.

Why publish fees at all?

Because cost is the first question most people have, and hiding it wastes everyone’s time. The ranges here are honest; your exact fee is confirmed in writing at the consultation.

What is included in the fee?

Records and scans, all appointments, the appliance, emergency visits, your first set of retainers, and follow-up for twelve months after treatment ends.

Do you take my insurance?

We are out-of-network with most PPO plans, which usually still reimburse a fixed orthodontic benefit. We verify your coverage and file all paperwork on your behalf.

Are payment plans interest-free?

Yes. Plans are spread across the length of treatment with no interest and no third-party financing company, after an initial payment.

Do you charge for the consultation?

New-patient consultations and standard records are complimentary. If CBCT imaging is needed, the fee is disclosed before the scan is taken.
