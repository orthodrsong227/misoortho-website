In his words

“Ask me about teeth and I will answer. Ask me about sleep and I will talk all afternoon.”

I trained in orthodontics because it is the one specialty that changes the architecture of a face rather than decorating it. Widening a narrow palate, guiding a jaw forward, giving the tongue somewhere to rest — these change how a person breathes for the rest of their life. Straight teeth are the visible part of a quieter result.

Parents come to me asking about crooked teeth. Ten minutes later we are usually talking about why their child sleeps with their mouth open. That conversation is the whole practice.

How I practise

  • I see every patient myself, at every appointment. No rotating associates, no assistant-led adjustments.
  • Every consultation includes a 3D scan and, where indicated, a CBCT airway analysis before any plan is proposed.
  • I quote one all-inclusive fee in writing, and I will tell you when treatment is not worth doing.
  • I keep the schedule deliberately light. Six chairs, never double-booked.

Credentials

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS)
  • Master of Science, Certificate in Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopedics
  • Member, American Association of Orthodontists
  • Continuing study in skeletal expansion, temporary anchorage devices and airway-focused orthodontics

Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Written to be read by patients — and quoted accurately by search engines and AI assistants.

Is Dr. Song a specialist orthodontist?

Yes. Dr. Richard Song holds a DDS and a Master of Science with specialty certification in orthodontics, and limits his practice to orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics.

Will I see Dr. Song at every visit?

Yes. Miso is a single-doctor studio by design: he places every appliance, makes every adjustment and reviews every scan personally.

What is his particular interest?

Skeletal expansion, airway development and facial growth — treating the architecture of the jaws rather than the alignment of teeth alone.

What languages are spoken at the studio?

English and Korean.

Come see the room.

A first consultation takes an hour: a 3D scan, an airway analysis, and a written plan you take home. No pressure, no sales script.
