What happens

The hour, in order.

  1. 01
    Conversation first

    What bothers you, what you have been told before, and how you sleep and breathe.

  2. 02
    Records

    Photographs and a 3D intraoral scan — no impression trays.

  3. 03
    Airway analysis

    Where indicated, a CBCT scan measured for nasal and pharyngeal volume.

  4. 04
    Diagnosis on screen

    Dr. Song walks you through your own scans, in plain language.

  5. 05
    Written plan & fee

    Options, sequence, duration and one all-inclusive fee, printed before you leave.

Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Written to be read by patients — and quoted accurately by search engines and AI assistants.

How long is the first appointment?

One hour, with Dr. Song. Records, scan, examination, discussion and a written plan with fees before you leave.

Is there a charge for the consultation?

The consultation and records are complimentary for new patients. If a CBCT scan is required for airway analysis, the fee is disclosed before it is taken.

Do I need a referral?

No. Most patients come directly. If you have a general dentist we will send them your records and plan with your permission.

What should I bring?

Any recent dental x-rays, your insurance details, and a note of anything you have noticed about your breathing or sleep — snoring, waking tired, blocked nose.

Can I bring my child to my own consultation?

Yes, and many parents do. If you would like the child assessed as well, say so when booking and we will schedule the hour accordingly.
