The studio

Ten rooms, none of them beige.

Walnut, hanji, travertine and hand-glazed celadon tile. We built a place that lowers your pulse at the door, because an orthodontic visit lasts an hour and you deserve to enjoy the room.

Visit usDirections & parking

Room by room

Reception

Backlit hanji, a curved walnut desk and a signage wall lit from behind.

Lounge

A single long linen bench under a cove light, instead of a row of waiting chairs.

Corridor

Arched walnut portals and an ink-wash landscape; the spine of the plan.

Open bay

Six chairs on walnut and travertine, divided by slatted screens rather than walls.

Consulting room

Glass-walled and quiet, for scans, plans and conversations that need privacy.

Doctor’s office

Where plans are written and parents ask their real questions.

Powder room

Hand-glazed celadon tile, plaster walls, an organic mirror.

Sterilisation

Deliberately visible: instrument processing you are welcome to watch.

Feature wall

Backlit stone and brass shelving; the first thing you see at the door.

Materials

Why it looks like this.

The palette is Korean: walnut, mulberry paper, stone, linen. The lighting is indirect everywhere, because nobody relaxes under a ceiling of downlights. Every arch you walk through is the same radius as the one in our logo lockup.

  1. 01
    Walnut

    Solid and veneered, slatted where sound needs softening.

  2. 02
    Hanji & stone

    Backlit panels that glow instead of glare.

  3. 03
    Travertine

    Underfoot in clinical zones, warm and quiet.

  4. 04
    Celadon

    Hand-glazed tile, the one cool colour in the building.
